## Usage and status

- Destination photographs appear in the homepage landscape sections, gallery and weather travel page with captions identifying the actual place.
- The Chaukhamba hero is captioned as a peak viewed from Tungnath; it is not represented as a view from the resort.
- Room/property photography remains an explicit notice until the resort owner supplies and approves photographs.
- No video or live webcam is included. No suitable authorised video or verified live feed was obtained for this change.
- No third-party photographs are used to represent guest reviews, cafes, rooms or resort facilities.
